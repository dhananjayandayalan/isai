# P5 plan — Harmony and arranger

## Goals

Every source type except carnatic gets chords and a playable two-hand arrangement. The
melody goes in the right hand; the left hand plays chord patterns for `easy`, `medium` or
`full`. Everything is rules (rule 7): deterministic, explainable and property-tested.
After P5, a friend can analyse a film song and practise a sensible two-hand version.
Built straight after P4, on the same branch line; planned now so one approval covers
both.

## Approval

The owner approved this plan on 2026-11-14 with P4's: one level per Song (no schema
change; `--level all` writes three files), and `hypothesis` as a dev dependency.

## Needs your approval

1. **This plan, and promoting the P5 spec from `draft` to `ready`.**
2. **Levels in the Song: the decision the spec asks for.** My recommendation: **one Song
   holds one arrangement for now, with no schema change.**
   - `--level easy|medium|full` (default `medium`), and `--level all` writes three files
     (`song.easy.song.json` and so on) from one analysis; only the arranger re-runs,
     because of the cache.
   - Why not `arrangements: [...]` now: before P8 every Song is a local file made with the
     audio at hand, so a re-run costs seconds. The real need appears with P8's library,
     where audio is deleted after upload. P8 can then add `arrangements` as an additive
     minor version, with the level switch in the UI, and its worker always writes all
     three.
   - The alternative is to add `arrangements` (schema 0.4.0) now, for a web change with no
     UI to use it yet.
3. **`hypothesis`** as a dev-only dependency (about 1 MB, MPL-2.0).
4. **ADR 0013, arranger:** levels, patterns, constraints and the one-arrangement-per-Song
   decision. Written *proposed*; you accept it after playing the arranged songs.
5. **Chords spike and ADR: deferred.** Comparing the baseline with a learned recogniser
   needs golden songs with `.lab` references, which don't exist yet. The template and
   Viterbi baseline needs no ADR (spec). The learned candidates are noted for later:
   BTC, which needs its licence checked, and madmom's CNN/CRF, which doesn't install here
   and is non-commercial.
6. **Sections: skipped** (optional in the spec). P6 already groups results by 4-bar
   phrases.

## Design decisions (mine)

- **Harmony** (`isai/harmony/`):
  - The `ChordRecogniser` protocol; the baseline `TemplateRecogniser`.
  - Beat-synchronous chroma from the harmonic stems ("other" + bass for full-mix; the audio
    for piano-solo, blended with the transcription's pitch classes).
  - Cosine scores against the templates in `theory/chords.py` (`maj`, `min`, `7`, `maj7`,
    `min7`, `dim`, `sus4`) and "no chord".
  - Viterbi with a key-aware prior: staying is cheapest, diatonic changes cost less than
    chromatic ones, and "no chord" only on near-silence.
  - Segments merged and snapped to beats. Bass from the lowest stable pitch class (the P4
    bass line, or the lowest transcribed notes); a slash chord when it isn't the root.
  - `confidence.chords` is the mean template margin.
- **a-cappella chords** (`harmony/from_melody.py`):
  - Per bar (half bar below 70 BPM), the diatonic triad covering the most melody duration
    on strong beats; ties go to I, IV, V, vi in that order.
  - A forced V–I (or IV–I) at phrase ends. Phrases end at rests of a beat or more, or
    every 4 bars.
  - At most one change per bar.
- **Labels** (`theory/chords.py`): one style, spelled to the key (`G`, `Em7`, `D/F#`,
  `Bb` in F major).
- **Arranger** (`isai/arrange/`), pure functions over (melody, chords, beats, downbeats,
  time signature, key, level):
  - **`melody.py`:**
    - Phrase-level octave moves into C4–C6.
    - The grid (1/8 or 1/16, or a triplet grid in compound feel) is chosen by the share of
      onsets each grid explains within 30 ms.
    - Grace notes under a 1/16 removed.
    - Note-rate caps per level: easy 2 per beat, medium 4, full none.
    - For piano-solo `--simplify`, the top voice is the highest note of each onset group,
      kept when it lasts at least a 1/16.
  - **`accompany.py`:** the patterns in the spec's table, per level and time signature.
    Inversions are chosen by least total movement from the previous voicing. Passing bass
    notes (full) come from the scale step between two chord roots, on the last eighth
    before the change.
  - **`constraints.py`:** a checker returning every violation (span ≤ a 9th, ≤ 4 notes per
    hand, a ≥ 2-semitone gap between hands, no same-key overlap, ≥ a 1/16, A0–C8), and a
    fixer applying the spec's order: left hand down an octave, then the right-hand phrase
    up, then dropping the inner left-hand notes. The pipeline runs the checker after
    fixing and fails loudly on anything left (a bug, not a song problem).
- **piano-solo** keeps the P4 transcription by default; `--simplify easy|medium` arranges
  it from its top voice and the recognised chords.
- **Determinism:** no randomness, stable sorts, rounding as in P4. Hypothesis
  property tests check every constraint, and identical output across runs, on generated
  chords and melodies.
- **MIDI out:** unchanged (a track per hand). Chord names go in as marker events, so a DAW
  shows them.

## Tests

- **`theory/chords.py`:** the templates, label spelling in sharp and flat keys, and slash
  chords.
- **Recogniser:** synthesised progressions (I–V–vi–IV, ii–V–I with sevenths, a slash-chord
  bass walk), with WCSR ≥ 0.9 on them; Viterbi prefers staying; "no chord" on silence.
- **`from_melody`:** a melody outlining I–IV–V–I gets those chords; a phrase-end cadence;
  at most one change per bar; waltz bars.
- **Arranger:** each level's pattern in 4/4 and 3/4; voice leading picks the closest
  inversion; octave moves keep phrases whole; quantising; grace-note removal; note-rate
  caps.
- **Constraints:** a hand-made case for each violation; the fixer's order; Hypothesis:
  zero violations and identical output for any generated input.
- **End to end with fakes:** each source type gives chords and two hands; byte-identical
  output twice.
- `arrange/`, `harmony/` (pure parts) and `theory/` ≥ 90% lines.

## Differences from the plan

- **Left-hand density per level is by rhythm, not note count.** When chords change every
  beat, medium's three-note blocks hold more notes than full's broken single notes. Full
  is still the busiest rhythm (onsets: easy ≤ medium < full).
- **Octave placement breaks ties upward** (most notes in C4–C6, then the smallest move,
  then nearest C5, then up), since the right hand carries the melody.
- **Forced cadences:** the span before a phrase end always becomes V or IV, even when the
  melody alone suggests I there. That's what the spec's "force a cadence" asks for.
- **The schema is unchanged** (one arrangement per Song, as approved), so `/schema-sync`
  has nothing to regenerate.
- **The chord spike and the golden-eval report** wait for golden `.lab` references and
  audio.
