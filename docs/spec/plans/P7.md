# P7 plan — Notation, display and settings

## Goals

The learner picks how notes are named (letters, solfège, swaras, Roman numerals for
chords) and sees them on the keys, on falling notes and in a chord lane, or reads a
scrolling grand staff instead. The sustain pedal sustains "play my notes" and shows on the
keyboard. A keyboard plugged in or out is noticed without "Scan again". A Devices screen
holds every setting, and all of them are saved. Web only, no schema change.

## Approval

is accepted, the Wurlitzer EP200 samples (CC BY 3.0) are approved subject to the spike

   [sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos),
   licence **CC BY 3.0** (the same as Salamander; attribution in the footer like the
   piano's). Commercial use is allowed, so it survives a public launch.
   - Rejected: jRhodes3d. Its samples are CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial), even though one
     mirror lists it as CC0.
   - Delivery: like the piano, a runtime URL (`VITE_EPIANO_SAMPLES_URL`). The default is a
     pinned jsDelivr URL of an existing MP3/OGG conversion of the same set, so nothing goes
     in git and no hosting is needed before P9.
   - First a small spike (`web/spikes/`, not shipped) picks about 15–20 samples (one
     velocity layer, every minor third, like the piano), measures the download (target
     under 4 MB) and checks the licence file travels with the copy. If no usable copy
     exists, I come back to you before converting files myself.
3. **Solfège:** do-based minor as the default (do re me fa sol le te), la-based as a
   setting. (The spec proposes this.)
4. **No notation library.** The staff is a canvas renderer that places notes by time; not
   printable sheet music.
5. **"App sound: Off (use my keyboard)"** — proposed meaning: Isai stops sounding *your*
   notes (today's "My keyboard's sound"); Isai's own parts keep playing on the grand
   piano. The electric piano choice applies to both your notes and Isai's parts. Say if
   "Off" should mean something else.
6. **Branch:** `p7-notation` is cut from `p6-performance`, because the Devices screen
   reuses P6's settings store and latency card. Nothing from P1, P2 or P6 is merged yet.

No new runtime dependency. No schema change.

## Design decisions (mine to make; listed so they can be questioned)

- **Roman numerals come from the chord's `root`, `quality` and `bass`,** not from parsing
  `label`. Upper case for major, augmented and dominant qualities, lower case for minor,
  diminished and half-diminished. Symbols `°`, `ø`, `+`; sevenths `7`, `M7`; inversions as
  figures (`6`, `64`, `65`, `43`, `42`); a bass that isn't a chord tone as `/` plus its
  degree. Degrees count from the key's own scale (major, or natural minor for minor keys,
  where `V` and `vii°` are allowed without an accidental). Other roots get `♭` or `♯`.
  `modal` keys count from the major scale. A major or dominant chord that isn't diatonic and
  whose root is a fifth above the next chord's diatonic root shows as `V/x` or `V7/x`.
- **Swara octaves:** madhya sthayi starts at the tonic in MIDI 60–71 (G major: Sa = G4).
  Below it is mandra (one dot below), below that anumandra (two dots); above it is tara (one
  dot above) and atitara (two dots). Shared positions follow the scale: with no R below, 2
  is R2 and 3 is G2; with R1 in the scale, 2 is G1; the same for D and N.
- **Spelling for a run of notes:** diatonic notes come from the key signature. A
  non-diatonic note is spelled raised when the next note of the same hand is higher and
  lowered when it is lower (lowered when it stands alone, raised for the leading tone in
  minor). Covers all 15 major and 15 minor key names. `theory/keys.ts` (added in the P6
  review) becomes part of this.
- **One notation setting**, as in the prototype: Letters, Solfège, Swaras, Roman numerals,
  Staff.
  - Letters, Solfège and Swaras name the keys and the falling notes. The chord lane shows
    chord labels for letters, and the root in the chosen system plus the quality for the
    other two (`Re m7`, `R2 m`).
  - Roman numerals: letters on the keys, numerals in the chord lane.
  - Staff: the staff replaces the falling notes, and the keys show letters.
  - Extra settings: octave numbers on letters (off), short or detailed swaras (short),
    do- or la-based minor (do).
- **One frame loop.** `PianoController` draws the stage canvas (falling notes or staff)
  and the keyboard canvas in the same `requestAnimationFrame` tick, from the same
  position and layout, so the columns line up at every keyboard size. React never sees a
  frame (rule 5).
- **Falling notes** look ahead 4 s by default (2–8 s setting). Notes off the keyboard
  become markers at the edge, as on the keys. The hand not being practised is drawn as a
  ghost, or hidden when "Show the arranged hand I'm not playing" is off. With
  `prefers-reduced-motion` the view moves once per beat instead of every frame.
- **Velocity curve** (soft, normal, hard) is `127 × (v / 127)^γ` with γ = 0.6, 1, 1.6,
  applied in the monitor only.
- **Electric piano** loads only when it is first chosen. The audio layer gives one
  switchable instrument, so the engine and the monitor don't change.
- **Settings become version 2.** Version 1 records migrate: `monitor: false` becomes app
  sound "Off". Unknown or broken fields fall back to defaults one by one.
- **Devices is a page** in the app's view switch (no router until P8). The keyboard
  panel stays mounted and hidden on the other pages (it holds the connection). Practice
  keeps a one-line connection status with a link to Devices.
- **Accessibility:** canvas text sizes are read from the root font size, so they follow
  the learner's font settings. A unit test checks the two hand colours against the three
  common colour-vision deficiencies (simulated, minimum difference) from `tokens.json`.

## Differences from the plan

- **Notes outside the key on a white key keep their plain letter** (B natural in F major,
  never Cb; G in G# minor, not F##). The plan had every outside note follow the direction
  of the line, which wrote B natural falling to Bb as "Cb". Direction now decides black
  keys only, and double sharps or flats only appear where the key itself has them.
- **`maj6` and `min6` read `add6`** in Roman numerals (`Iadd6`), because a bare `6` is the
  first-inversion figure.
- **"Note names" is also on the Practice screen**, as a select next to the keyboard size,
  so the learner can switch between swaras, Roman numerals and the staff without leaving
  the song. It saves the same setting as the Devices screen.
- **Hiding the other hand hides it on the keys too**, not only on the stage.
- **Names on the keys are left out when they don't fit the key** (88 keys on a narrow
  screen). P1 always drew the C labels, overlapping their neighbours.
- **The chord lane** runs down the left edge of the falling notes (each chord at its start,
  the one sounding at the bottom) and along the top of the staff.
- **The Devices screen's Done button names where it goes** ("Back to practice", "Back to
  song", "Back to results"); Practice stays mounted underneath, so nothing is lost.
- **A replugged keyboard reconnects without a click**, so its sound may wait for the next
  click if the browser had suspended audio (the next click resumes it, ADR 0004).
- **Reduced motion is tested at unit level only** (the stepped position and the
  controller); jsdom has no `matchMedia`.
- **Review fixes** (`reports/review-P7.md`): Done on the Devices screen no longer returns
  to another song's result; key names follow the font size; chord tones are spelled by the
  hand's next note; a keyboard that reconnects by itself says so.
- **Owner: `.env.example`** needs `VITE_EPIANO_SAMPLES_URL=` next to
  `VITE_PIANO_SAMPLES_URL=` (Claude can't edit `.env.*`).
