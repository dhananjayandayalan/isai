# P4 plan — Transcription

## Goals

The pipeline writes notes. `isai analyse song.mp3 --type piano-solo` gives a two-hand Song
you can train on. `--type full-mix` separates stems and writes the vocal melody as
right-hand notes, keeping the bass line as a cached artifact for P5. `--type a-cappella`
writes the sung melody and `tonicHz`. Pipeline only, apart from the web accepting
schema 0.3.0.

## Approval

The owner approved this plan on 2026-11-14, with the P4 and P5 specs promoted to
`ready`: torch with the CUDA build on this laptop, the default models and dependencies
(torchaudio Hybrid Demucs, ByteDance piano transcription, torchcrepe), schema 0.3.0, and
exit code 7. The ADRs stay proposed until golden numbers exist.

## Order: why P4 starts before P3 closes

On 2026-11-14 the owner asked for P4 and P5 to go ahead before P3 closes: a Song with no
notes can't be practised. P3's open items (the beat-tracking ADR 0009, its golden-eval
report) wait only on the golden audio. P4 builds on what P3 has merged, and nothing in P4
depends on which beat tracker wins.

The same constraint shapes P4: **the model spikes need the golden audio too**. So, as in
P3, each model sits behind its protocol with a sensible default, chosen on licence,
dependencies and published accuracy. Each spike harness is built and smoke-tested on
synthesised audio. The ADRs record the default as *proposed* and are finalised with
golden numbers when the audio arrives.

## Design decisions (mine; listed so they can be questioned)

- **The stage graph per source type:**
  - `piano-solo`: ingest → rhythm → piano → tonality (audio chroma + notes) → hands.
  - `full-mix`: ingest → separate → rhythm (the mix) → tonality (the mix without drums
    and vocals) → melody (vocals) and bass line (bass stem) → tonality refinement →
    hands (all right).
  - `a-cappella`: ingest → melody → rhythm (as now; weak on voice, flagged by its
    confidence) → tonality from the notes, with `tonicHz` → hands (all right).

  Each is a cached runner stage with its own version.
- **Models** (`isai/models.py`): one registry of name, URL, SHA-256 and size.
  - `isai models fetch [name]` downloads and verifies; analysis never downloads on its own
    (the P8 worker image will bake them in).
  - `ISAI_MODEL_DIR` overrides the folder.
  - `--device auto|cpu|cuda`, where auto means CUDA when available; the runner logs which
    device it used.
- **Separation** (`isai/separate/`):
  - The mono mix is duplicated to two channels and run in fixed 10 s segments with 25%
    overlap. No random shifts, so it's deterministic.
  - Stems are cached as mono float32 at 22.05 kHz, about 85 MB per 4-minute song in
    `.cache/`.
  - `--keep-stems` writes WAVs to `<out>/stems/`. A test checks that nothing else is
    written outside `.cache/` without it.
- **Piano** (`isai/transcribe/piano.py`): the `PianoTranscriber` protocol; the ByteDance
  adapter returns notes and pedal spans. Post-processing (`transcribe/piano_post.py`, pure)
  is as the spec lists:
  - offsets extended under the pedal and capped at the next onset of the same key;
  - notes under 30 ms dropped;
  - velocity clamped to 1–127;
  - duplicates removed.
- **Melody** (`isai/transcribe/melody.py`): the `PitchTracker` protocol gives f0 and voicing
  every 10 ms. Segmentation (`transcribe/segment.py`, pure) follows the spec step by step:
  hysteresis voicing, median filter, cents against the refined tuning, semitone quantising
  with a stability window, 80 ms minimum, 60 ms gap merging, velocity from RMS. The bass
  line uses the same path on the bass stem, cached as an artifact for P5.
- **Tonality refinement:** the key score is `(1 − w) · chroma + w · note histogram`
  (duration-weighted). w starts at 0.5 and is tuned on the golden set. `tonicHz` is the
  tonic pitch class's mean f0 in the singer's most-used octave.
- **Hands** (`isai/arrange/hands.py`): onset groups, then a dynamic-programming pass over
  beats choosing a split point per beat. The cost is: notes outside a 10th of their hand's
  centre, split movement between beats, and distance from middle C. A chord is never split
  across a crossing. Melody-only input goes all right.
- **`meta.confidence.notes`:** the tracker's mean voicing confidence (melody), or the mean
  onset probability (piano).
- **Determinism:** fixed seeds, `torch.use_deterministic_algorithms` where it is
  supported, times rounded to 1 ms, velocities to integers, cents to 0.1. Determinism is
  tested on CPU (`slow`); the GPU difference is measured and documented.

## Tests

- **Segmentation (synthesised f0 tracks):** steady notes, vibrato (±50 cents at 6 Hz)
  that stays one note, a slide between two notes, unvoiced gaps, a short gap merge, a
  blip under 80 ms dropped, detuned input, and silence. ≥ 90% coverage.
- **Piano post-processing:** pedal extension, a cap at the same key's next onset, short
  notes, velocity clamping, duplicates.
- **Hands:** a scale crossing middle C, a wide left-hand arpeggio, a chord on the split,
  a hand crossing, melody-only input, an empty input. `arrange/` ≥ 90%.
- **Tonality refinement:** notes alone find the key of an I–IV–V–I; a weighted mix; and
  `tonicHz` on synthesised singing at known shrutis.
- **Fakes for every protocol**, so the stage graph, cache, flags and exit codes are tested
  without torch. Real models run only in `slow` tests: a synthesised piano piece through
  ByteDance, a synthesised voice through torchcrepe, and a 2-stem synthetic mix through
  HDemucs.
- **Rule 8:** with and without `--keep-stems`, list every file written.
- **Models:** a checksum mismatch and a missing file both exit 7, offline.
- **Schema:** 0.1.0–0.3.0 examples load on both sides.

## Exit criteria (from spec)

- [ ] Model ADRs accepted; dependencies and schema change approved
- [ ] piano-solo: a real recording becomes a two-hand Song that plays and trains correctly in the web app (owner, by ear and by hand)
- [ ] full-mix and a-cappella: a right-hand melody Song plays in the web app; a-cappella has `tonicHz`
- [ ] Segmentation, hand assignment and post-processing are unit-tested on synthetic inputs; `arrange/` and `theory/` ≥ 90%
- [ ] Determinism test passes on CPU (and on GPU, or the GPU difference is documented and rounded away)
- [ ] No stems or audio written outside `.cache/` unless `--keep-stems` is passed (test)
- [ ] `/golden-eval` report committed with the new note and melody metrics; no regressions in key or tempo, or explained
- [ ] Runtime per stage reported for CPU and GPU in the plan
- [ ] Pipeline ruff, pyright and pytest pass
