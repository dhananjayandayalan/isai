# P2 plan — Training mode

## Goals

On the practice screen a learner picks Listen or Training, a hand (right, left or both), a
speed (40–120%) and optionally a bar range to loop. In Training, playback runs to the next
notes for that hand, holds with those keys lit ("Waiting for G4 B4") until they are played,
then carries on. A metronome and a one-bar count-in can be switched on. One practice
engine does all of this, with a policy per mode, so P6 can add performance mode without
touching steps, matching, speed or loop. No schema change, no pipeline work, no new
dependencies.

**Audio — `web/src/audio/`**
- `types.ts`: `Transport.pause(time?)`, `Transport.stop(time?)`, `Transport.lookahead`;
  `LoadedAudio.metronome`.
- `tone.ts`: the above, plus a short synth click as the metronome `Instrument`.
- `FakeTransport.ts`: an audio clock that keeps running while paused, `wait()`, `lookahead`.

**Engine — `web/src/engine/practice/`**
- `bars.ts`: bars from beats and the time signature (or 2-second chunks with no beats);
  accepts downbeats when the schema has them.
- `steps.ts`: `buildSteps(notes, { hands, tolerance, range, playable })`.
- `matcher.ts`: `StepMatcher` (held keys, early grace, exact or any-octave, wrong notes).
- `engine.ts`: `PracticeEngine` (policies, timeline, windowed scheduling, holds, loop,
  speed, metronome, count-in, per-key feedback for the piano).
- `engine/player.ts` is removed; its cases move to the practice-engine tests.

**Piano — `web/src/piano/`**
- `KeyboardRenderer.ts`: current-step keys lit with a pill, next-step keys outlined, the
  other hand dimmed, a correct or wrong mark on the key you pressed.
- `PianoController.ts`: reads the engine's per-key arrays every frame.
- `design/system/tokens.css`: two fixed piano tokens for the correct and wrong marks.

**Page**
- `Practice.tsx`: mode, hands, other hand, speed, loop, metronome, count-in, the
  "Waiting for …" readout and step counter (written through refs), transport, keyboard.
- `App.tsx`: song picker, summary, sound states, MIDI panel.
